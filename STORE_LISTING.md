# Pak-Prep — Store Listing Draft

## Short description
Pakistan government job past papers and exam preparation for BPS-5 to BPS-22.

## Full description
Prepare for government recruitment examinations across Pakistan with Pak-Prep. Browse job-wise past-paper categories by recruiting body, province, department, post and BPS scale. Study separate subjects including English, General Knowledge, Pakistan Studies, Islamic Studies, Everyday Science, Mathematics, Computer, Current Affairs, Urdu and Pedagogy. Improve English with Dawn article links and independent Urdu vocabulary notes, synonyms, antonyms and essay-ready examples.

Coverage directory is designed for federal, Punjab, Sindh, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Balochistan, AJK and Gilgit-Baltistan recruitment. Individual paper availability depends on verified source records.
