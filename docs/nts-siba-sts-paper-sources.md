# NTS and SIBA-STS paper sources

Pak-Prep now exposes the official provider archives from the Papers tab.

## NTS (National Testing Service)

- Official results / answer-key / test archive: https://www.nts.org.pk/new/Allresults.php
- Official candidate and test listings: https://www.nts.org.pk/new/candidates.php
- Statistical Officer sample paper (2024 listing): https://www.nts.org.pk/Test%26Products/Lists/04_24/SBP_21_April_2024_FL/SAMPLE%20PAPER.pdf
- English sample paper: https://nts.org.pk/Test%26Products/Lists/1220/APS_25Dec20_FL/SAMPLE%20PAPER%20ENGLISH.pdf
- Supreme Court of Pakistan written test sample-paper index: https://www.nts.org.pk/Test%26Products/Lists/012020/SC_P2_02Feb2020_FL/Sample/Sample.php

## SIBA-STS (Sukkur IBA Testing Services)

- Official STS sample-paper archive: https://www.iba-suk.edu.pk/sts/sample-papers/
- Police Constable sample question paper (official SIBA-STS PDF): https://apps.iba-suk.edu.pk/dashboard-admission-sts-hr/down/sts_documents/20240809130440286.pdf

The SIBA-STS archive page lists sample papers including Session Court Malir Karachi, District & Session Court Karachi, SEF/SELD students, JEST, ECT and finance/audit recruitment roles.

## Import status and limitations

- The app currently links to the official provider archives and selected direct sample PDFs.
- This is not a complete imported archive of every historical NTS or SIBA-STS exam paper.
- The NTS public archive mixes test listings, results and answer keys; each listing must be opened and inspected to establish whether it links to an actual question paper, sample paper, answer key or only a result.
- The SIBA-STS page located is explicitly a sample-paper index; sample papers must not be represented as completed past examinations.
- Only exact, verified question-paper URLs should be entered as historical past-paper records. Keep answer keys and sample papers in their own resource types.
- Do not download/rehost or copy proprietary third-party papers without a licence or written permission. Link to the official source otherwise.
