# Content collection and verification policy

1. Record the original source URL, publisher/recruiting body, exact post title, BPS, exam year/date and language for every paper.
2. Import full paper files or question text only where public-domain status, a reuse licence or explicit written authorization is documented.
3. For third-party collections such as TestPoint, use source links and independently verify provenance; do not scrape or mirror a complete database without permission.
4. Dawn articles are linked to their publisher. Do not republish full article text. Create original summaries and vocabulary notes and check every word meaning against reliable dictionaries.
5. A paper is not published until metadata is checked, the file opens, duplicate checks pass and answers are independently verified.
6. Keep an audit trail for corrections and takedown requests.
7. Mark unverified questions clearly; do not represent generated practice questions as official past-paper questions.
